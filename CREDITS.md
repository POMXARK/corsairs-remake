# Credits

The built assets are committed in `assets/` and `game/resource/` and rebuilt by `run.sh` from free sources:
`tools/fetch_assets.sh` and `engine/demos/dialog/fetch_assets.sh` download the fonts and the menu background,
`tools/make_assets.py` converts them to textures and `fonts.ini`, and the sea, the ships, the island, the particles,
the cannonball, the sounds, the music and the HUD textures are generated by the engine demos
(`engine/demos/{sea,ship,particles,sound,battle,island,hud}/make_*.py`).

| Asset | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background: *The Ninth Wave* (1850) | Ivan Aivazovsky | Public domain | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hovhannes_Aivazovsky_-_The_Ninth_Wave_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg) |
| Font: Old Standard Bold (menu, battle log) | Alexey Kryukov | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/OFL.txt`) | [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oldstandardtt) |
| Font: PT Serif Regular and Bold (HUD speeds, compass letters) | ParaType | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/ptserif/OFL.txt`) | [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ptserif) |
| Tune: "Drunken Sailor" | traditional | Public domain | arranged and synthesized by `engine/demos/sound/make_sound.py` |
| Generated models, textures, sounds | Storm Engine fork | same as the engine | the `make_*.py` generators above |

Button frames and the cursor are drawn by `tools/make_assets.py`.
